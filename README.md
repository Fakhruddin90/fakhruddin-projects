## Data Science & Machine Learning

**Case study: bank marketing analytics.** These nine end-to-end projects follow one scenario: a data science consultancy helping a retail bank use its customer data to improve marketing campaigns. Together they cover the full workflow, from raw data through modeling to deployment.

| # | Project | What it does | Tech | Highlight |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Customer Data ETL Pipeline](https://github.com/Fakhruddin90/customer-data-etl-pipeline) | Consolidates customer data from CSV and SQLite into one clean master table | Python, pandas, SQLite | Model-ready master dataset |
| 2 | [Consumer Loan Complaint Text Processing](https://github.com/Fakhruddin90/consumer-loan-complaint-text-processing-nlp) | Cleans complaint narratives with tokenization, POS tagging and lemmatization | spaCy, NLTK | Reusable text-cleaning function |
| 3 | [EDA and Preprocessing of Customer Data](https://github.com/Fakhruddin90/eda-and-preprocessing-of-customer-data) | Explores, imputes, engineers and reduces features | pandas, seaborn, scikit-learn | PCA: 17 features → 2 components |
| 4 | [Fine-Tuned Complaint Summarization Model](https://github.com/Fakhruddin90/fine-tuned-complaint-summarization-model) | Fine-tunes a T5 transformer to summarize customer complaints | Hugging Face Transformers, PyTorch | Fine-tuned T5-small seq2seq model |
| 5 | [Term Deposit Prediction](https://github.com/Fakhruddin90/term-deposit-prediction-classification) | Predicts which customers will subscribe to a term deposit | scikit-learn, XGBoost, SMOTE | **0.91** CV accuracy (XGBoost) |
| 6 | [Customer Total Balance Prediction](https://github.com/Fakhruddin90/customer-total-balance-prediction-regression) | Predicts a customer's total balance with regression models | scikit-learn, XGBoost | Compared 5 regressors on R², MAE and MSE |
| 7 | [Customer Segmentation](https://github.com/Fakhruddin90/customer-segmentation-clustering) | Segments customers into marketing target groups | scikit-learn, Yellowbrick, Streamlit | 12 segments you can interpret |
| 8 | [Model Demonstration Web App](https://github.com/Fakhruddin90/model-demonstration-web-app) | Serves all three models with live charts in the browser | Flask, Jinja, matplotlib | Interactive model demo |
| 9 | [Automated ML Pipeline](https://github.com/Fakhruddin90/automated-ml-pipeline) | Searches scalers, PCA and models automatically with GridSearchCV | scikit-learn Pipelines | **0.89** CV accuracy (best pipeline) |
